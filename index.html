English
One thing I took home from church today is this: not every problem needs an audience. Sometimes, when someone hurts us, the first thing we want to do is tell everybody what happened. But today's Gospel reminded me that sometimes the better thing is to go to that person, talk about it, and try to make peace. We all make mistakes. We all offend people sometimes, whether we mean to or not. Correction should not always come with embarrassment, sometimes, it should come with love. And honestly, I think we need more of that in our homes, friendships, marriages, workplaces, and even here on Facebook. Before you judge someone, talk to them. Before you expose them, try to correct them. Before you walk away, ask yourself if reconciliation is still possible. Love your neighbour as yourself, that was one message from today's Mass that really stayed with me.
